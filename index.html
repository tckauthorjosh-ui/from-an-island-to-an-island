The question that started it

Do you know the way?

St. John's, Newfoundland — where the question started

What began as a lighthearted joke between friends eventually became a much deeper question: do we actually know "the way" in our lives?

Is it the path others expect us to follow — attending university, building a career, finding a partner, buying a home, and settling into a life that appears certain?

During a sabbatical year in Taiwan, Josh (Buddy) reflects on identity, belonging, faith, grief, and the search for "the way" — through conversations with friends, fellow travellers, and those who call Taiwan home.

Part travel memoir, part dialogue, it follows a third-culture kid's search for meaning, belonging, and a sense of home. Will he find "the way" by the end of his sabbatical?

About the voices behind the book
Josh Wong (left) and Ivan Okello — author and contributor

Josh Wong

Author

A Canadian third-culture kid and polyglot with ties in Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, and Hong Kong. In 2025, Josh went on a gap year to reflect on identity, belonging, and the way. He enjoys "having a time" at a kitchen party with Newfoundland music, and getting happily lost in a Taipei night market with friends and food. This is his first book.

Ivan Okello

Contributor

Ivan grew up in Uganda before relocating to Atlantic Canada, completing his education at Makerere University and Dalhousie University. A longtime friend of Josh, Ivan enjoys travelling, meeting people from different cultures, and thoughtful conversation. He hosts the podcast Dear Lads, available on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.
